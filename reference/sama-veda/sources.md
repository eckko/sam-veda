## Conversion notes

- S1 is ITRANS. Converted with `indic_transliteration` (`ITRANS` to `DEVANAGARI`) by `tools/parse_itx.py`; text is NFC.
- Scheme consequences worth knowing: `.N` becomes chandrabindu (ँ, 885 times) and `M`/`.n`-style anusvara stays anusvara (ं); `R^I` becomes long vocalic r (ॄ, 6 times, e.g. `mAtR^IH`); `3` becomes the pluta sign (३); `.a` becomes avagraha (ऽ). Whether the typist meant `.N` as chandrabindu throughout is a question for the verification stage (ISSUES.md).
- The original `sv-kauthuma.itx` is not stored in this repo (owner's decision 2026-10-17, because of the licence header). To re-run the parser, download it from the URL above, check the sha256, and run `python3 -I reference/sama-veda/tools/parse_itx.py sv-kauthuma.itx --write reference/sama-veda`.
- Cross-check with S2: `python3 -I reference/sama-veda/tools/crosscheck_s2.py reference/sama-veda <S2 json>`.
- Owner decision needed: the licence of S1 allows personal study and research; check it before this repo or anything derived from it is made public or used commercially.
