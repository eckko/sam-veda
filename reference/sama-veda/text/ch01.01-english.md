01.01.1a | Come, O Agni, being praised, to the feast, to the offering of the oblation; sit down as the Hotṛ priest on the sacred grass.
01.01.1b | ~
01.01.2a | You, O Agni, are the Hotṛ priest of all sacrifices, placed by the gods among the human people.
01.01.2b | ~
01.01.3a | We choose Agni as messenger, the Hotṛ priest, the all-knowing, the very skilful one of this sacrifice.
01.01.3b | ~
01.01.4a | May Agni, kindled, bright and offered to, seeking wealth, strike the obstacles with admiring praise.
01.01.4b | ~
01.01.5a | I praise your dearest guest, like a dear friend; O Agni, like a chariot to be gained.
01.01.5b | ~
01.01.6a | Protect us, O Agni, with your great powers, from all ill-will and from the hatred of the mortal.
01.01.6b | ~
01.01.7a | Come, O Agni; let me speak to you thus other songs; may you grow strong with these drops.
01.01.7b | ~
01.01.8a | May Vatsa draw your mind hither even from the highest abode; O Agni, I desire you with a song.
01.01.8b | ~
01.01.9a | Atharvan churned you forth, O Agni, from the lotus, from the head of every sacrificer.
01.01.9b | ~
01.01.10a | O Agni, bring us what is radiant for great help; for you are a god, for us to see.
01.01.10b | ~
01.01.11a | Homage to you, O Agni, for strength; O god, the peoples praise you. Crush the enemy with your powers.
01.01.11b | ~
01.01.12a | Your messenger, the all-knowing, the carrier of oblations, the immortal, the best sacrificer, I adorn with a song.
01.01.12b | ~
01.01.13a | The kindred songs of the maker of oblations, pointing to you, have taken their stand beside you, at the front of Vāyu.
01.01.13b | ~
01.01.14a | To you, O Agni, day by day, O illuminer of the night, with thought, bringing homage, we come.
01.01.14b | ~
01.01.15a | O you who awaken to praise, attend to that: for Rudra, worthy of sacrifice in every clan, a hymn beautiful to behold.
01.01.15b | ~
01.01.16a | To that lovely rite you are invoked, for the drinking; come, O Agni, with the Maruts.
01.01.16b | ~
01.01.17a | To praise you, Agni, like a horse with a tail, with homage, you who rule supreme over the rites.
01.01.17b | ~
01.01.18a | Like Aurva and Bhṛgu and like Apnavāna, I call the bright Agni, whose dwelling is the sea.
01.01.18b | ~
01.01.19a | The mortal, kindling Agni with his mind, may follow the prayer; he kindles Agni with the radiant ones.
01.01.19b | ~
01.01.20a | Then indeed they see the daily light of the ancient seed, which is kindled on high in the sky.
01.01.20b | ~
01.01.21a | Towards your Agni, who grows among the rites, the most manifold, towards the mighty descendant.
01.01.21b | ~
01.01.22a | May Agni with his sharp flame subdue every devourer; may Agni win wealth for us.
01.01.22b | ~
01.01.23a | O Agni, be gracious; you are great, you who go to the people devoted to the gods, to the sacred grass to sit.
01.01.23b | ~
01.01.24a | O Agni, protect us from distress; O god, ageless one, burn against the one who would harm us with your hottest (flames).
01.01.24b | ~
01.01.25a | O Agni, yoke those horses of yours, O god, the good ones, which, swift, carry readily.
01.01.25b | ~
01.01.26a | Approaching (you), O lord of the clans, we set down you, the radiant one, rich in good heroes, O Agni to whom oblations are offered.
01.01.26b | ~
01.01.27a | Agni is the head and the summit of heaven, and the lord of the earth; this one quickens the seeds of the waters.
01.01.27b | ~
01.01.28a | Declare well among the gods, O Agni, this newest gāyatra song of ours, this gift.
01.01.28b | ~
01.01.29a | Gopavana has brought you forth with a song, O Agni, O Aṅgiras; as that one, O purifier, hear our call.
01.01.29b | ~
01.01.30a | Agni, the lord of strength, the seer, has gone around the oblations, bestowing treasures on the giver.
01.01.30b | ~
01.01.31a | The rays carry up that god Jātavedas, the sun, for all to see.
01.01.31b | ~
01.01.32a | At the rite praise Agni the seer, whose ordinances are true, the god who drives away affliction.
01.01.32b | ~
01.01.33a | May the goddesses be a blessing for us, for our aid; may they be a blessing for our drinking; may they flow towards us with welfare and well-being.
01.01.33b | ~
01.01.34a | Whose prayers do you now quicken in abundance, O lord of the good, whose songs are pleasing to you?
01.01.34b | ~
01.01.35a | At every sacrifice and with every song, for you, for the skilful Agni, let me proclaim forth the immortal Jātavedas, dear like a friend.
01.01.35b | ~
01.01.36a | Protect us, O Agni, with one, and protect with the second; protect with three songs, O lord of strengths; protect with four, O good one.
01.01.36b | ~
01.01.37a | With your great flames, O Agni, with your bright blaze, O god, being kindled in Bharadvāja, O youngest, O purifier, shine richly.
01.01.37b | ~
01.01.38a | In you, O well-invoked Agni, may the patrons be dear: the bountiful controllers of the people, who distribute a herd of cows.
01.01.38b | ~
01.01.39a | O Agni, O praiser, lord of the clans, O god, burning the demons; ever-present, O master of the house, you are great, the protector of heaven, the guardian of the house.
01.01.39b | ~
01.01.40a | O Agni, O immortal, bring the shining, brilliant bounty of the dawn; O Jātavedas, bring to the giver today the gods who awake at dawn.
01.01.40b | ~
01.01.41a | O brilliant one, O good one, urge bounties on to us with your help; O Agni, you are the charioteer of this wealth; find a firm footing for our offspring.
01.01.41b | ~
01.01.42a | You indeed are widespread, O Agni, O protector, true seer; the inspired singers, the devout, worship you, O kindled one, O shining one.
01.01.42b | ~
01.01.43a | Bring to us, O Agni, O purifier, praiseworthy wealth that increases vigour; and give us, O near one, much-desired, most glorious (wealth) by good leading.
01.01.43b | ~
01.01.44a | He who distributes all goods, the Hotṛ priest, gladdening among the people — to him, like the first cups of honey, may hymns be offered forth to Agni.
01.01.44b | ~
01.01.45a | With this homage I call for you Agni, the offspring of strength, the dear, most attentive, the attendant, of good rites, the immortal messenger of all.
01.01.45b | ~
01.01.46a | You lie in the woods, in the mothers; mortals kindle you. Unwearied, you carry the oblation for the maker of oblations; then indeed you shine among the gods.
01.01.46b | ~
01.01.47a | He who is the best finder of the way has been seen, in whom they have placed their ordinances; may our songs approach well the newborn Agni, the increaser of the noble one.
01.01.47b | ~
01.01.48a | Agni is placed in front at the recitation; the pressing stones and the sacred grass are at the rite. With a verse I go to the Maruts, O lord of the prayer; the gods' desirable help.
01.01.48b | ~
01.01.49a | Praise Agni for help with songs, him of keen flame. Famed Agni for wealth, O Purumīḍha; the men, Agni, for good shining, shelter.
01.01.49b | ~
01.01.50a | Hear, O Agni of the listening ear, with the gods, the bearers who go together; at the rite may Mitra and Aryaman sit on the sacred grass with those who come early.
01.01.50b | ~
01.01.51a | Forth, Agni the god of Divodāsa, like Indra in might, has turned about along mother Earth and has stood in the shelter of the vault of heaven.
01.01.51b | ~
01.01.52a | Whether from the earth or from heaven, from the great luminous realm; grow with this body and this song; O very wise one, fill my offspring.
01.01.52b | ~
01.01.53a | Desiring the woods, when you went to the mothers, the waters; that return of yours, O Agni, is not to be forgotten, that, being far away, you were here.
01.01.53b | ~
01.01.54a | Manu has set you down, O Agni, as a light for the people everywhere; you shone among the Kaṇvas, O one born of truth, sprinkled, whom the peoples revere.
