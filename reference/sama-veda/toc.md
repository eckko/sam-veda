# Sama Veda Samhita, Kauthuma Shakha (सामवेदसंहिता, कौथुमशाखा)

- Author / compiler: none named; Vedic Samhita of the Sama Veda, Kauthuma recension (the file's title names the Kauthuma Shakha)
- Subject and purpose of the book: the collection of verses (mantras) that are sung in the Sama Veda; the file arranges them in two main books (Purvarcika and Uttararcika) with two short books between them (inferred from the file's own headings)
- Base edition: sv-kauthuma.itx, Sanskrit Documents, typed and proofread by Anshuman Pandey, latest update 28 August 2021 (source S1 in sources.md). The printed edition behind it is not stated in the file
- Mapping: part = arcika (as headed in the file), chapter = ardha (half of a prapathaka; one chapter for the two short books), verse = mantra, numbered continuously 1 to 1875 as in the file
- Totals: 4 parts, 36 chapters, 1875 verses extracted (traditional: 1875)

Notes on the structure:

- Kanda boundaries do not fall on chapter boundaries. Agneya verses 1-114 (chapters 01.01 to 01.03), Aindra 115-466 (01.03 to 01.10), Pavamana 467-585 (01.10 to 01.12); the Aranya book (part 02) is headed `AraNya kANDam`.
- The file groups verses in tens (Purvarcika, Aranya, Mahanamnya: coordinate `DDEE`) and in threes (Uttararcika: sukta `DD`, verse `EE`). These groupings are not written into the text files.
- Uttararcika prapathakas 1-5 have two ardhas each, prapathakas 6-9 have three (the file says `tR^itIyo.ardhaH`).
- The rishi, chandas and devata lines that the file prints before the first eight suktas of the Uttararcika are kept in `notes/annotations.md` (key: the first line of the verse that follows).
